# Community code of conduct

p5js live is for people making, performing, sharing, and discussing live-coded visuals.
Participation should make that work safer and more welcoming.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful of different backgrounds, identities, skill levels, tools, and
  creative practices.
- Offer criticism that is specific, constructive, and directed at the work.
- Credit visual, musical, and technical influences.
- Respect boundaries, consent, privacy, and the context in which work is shared.
- Help keep performances, issues, reviews, and discussions accessible to newcomers.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, unwanted sexual attention, deliberate
intimidation, sustained disruption, doxxing, and publishing another person's private
information are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report community-conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through their
GitHub profile. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical. Maintainers
may edit or remove contributions, restrict participation, or take other proportionate
action to protect the community.

This code applies in repository spaces and in public events where someone is
representing p5js live or its community.
