ALGOLAB

AUDIO-REACTIVE VISUAL LIVE CODING IN THE BROWSER

THE UNIVERSITY OF TEXAS AT AUSTIN

AlgoLab is a browser-based visual instrument from the Department of Arts and Entertainment Technologies at The University of Texas at Austin. Build visuals with JavaScript patches, arrange them into a scene, and change the composition while it runs.

PATCH
An object, function, or class instance that knows how to draw.
SCENE
An ordered array of patches that AlgoLab draws together.
LIVE
Edit a block and press Cmd/Ctrl + Enter.
